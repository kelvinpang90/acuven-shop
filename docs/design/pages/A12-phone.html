4 · Free shippingRM0.00 to RM999,999.99; 0 means every order ships free. Thresholds use the amount after item discounts.
Combining with coupons and points
When a member uses a coupon or points, the system compares the order with and without the sale and uses whichever is better for them. Unticked options can't be used together with the sale.
