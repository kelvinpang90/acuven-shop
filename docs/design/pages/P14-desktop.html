Privacy

This whole site is a demonstration; no real orders are fulfilled.

This site is a demo store. We collect only what is needed to run the demo, and nothing is really paid for or delivered.

What we collect

For orders: recipient name, phone number, country, region, address and postcode. For members: mobile number and, if you set one, a securely hashed password. We do not ask for email or ID documents.

You may use fictional shipping details. The exception is a Malaysian or Singapore mobile number entered at checkout, which must be able to receive our verification SMS.

How long we keep it

Shipping details are kept long-term together with the order's items, amounts and status. They are not deleted or anonymised, including after a member account is deleted.

Member mobile numbers, including accounts created automatically at checkout, are kept until you delete your account; inactive accounts are not deleted automatically. SMS verification records are kept briefly to prevent abuse.

After you delete your account, copies of your mobile number and password hash made before the deletion may remain in our database backups for a period that has not yet been determined.

Who can see it

After you place a guest order or look up an order, this browser gets access to that order for 30 minutes. After 30 minutes, or in another browser, look the order up again with its order number and phone number.

Anyone who knows both the order number and the phone number can view the full shipping details. Because shipping details are kept long-term, this stays possible for as long as the order exists.

SMS and logs

Checkout verification for Malaysian and Singapore numbers, registration, SMS login, password reset and account deletion send a real SMS through our SMS provider.

Our logs are kept for up to 30 days and do not contain your name, full phone number, address or password.
