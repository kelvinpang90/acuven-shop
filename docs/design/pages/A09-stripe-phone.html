DEMOAdmin — demo store. Actions here never move real money or goods.
Site settings
SMS verification When on, Malaysian and Singapore numbers verify by SMS at checkout and become members, and registration, SMS login, password reset and account deletion use SMS. When off, no SMS is sent: visitors who are not logged in check out as guests whatever their number, registration, SMS login and password reset are paused, and members confirm account deletion with their password or, without one, while logged in.
Turning this on sends real SMS messages, which cost money. Turn it on only after the SMS service has been set up and checked.
Payment method
Stripe test mode can't be selected: its test secret key or webhook signing secret is missing or invalid. Only test keys are accepted, never live keys.
Simulated: customers pick an outcome on the store's own demo payment page. Stripe test mode: customers pay on Stripe's test payment page with test cards or other test methods offered there (such as FPX or GrabPay), and no real money moves; for those orders, approving a refund also sends a refund request to Stripe's test mode, and its status is shown in the refund details. Orders below Stripe's minimum amount still use simulated payment. Stripe payment sessions already started keep being handled through Stripe even if you switch back.
